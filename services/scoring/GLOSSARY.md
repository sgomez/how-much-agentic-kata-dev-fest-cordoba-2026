# Scoring

The points of a game. It works out what every player earns in each round, from the estimates, the solution and the impostor's verdict, and adds them up.

## Language

**Points**: What a player earns in one round. Every point in the game, the impostor's included, is scoring's. _Avoid_: Score

**Closest**: The estimate or estimates at the smallest absolute distance from the solution, whether over or under. Only the closest estimates earn placement points: 3 each. _Avoid_: Nearest, winner, best guess

**Exact hit**: An estimate equal to the solution. It earns 2 points on top of the 3 for being closest. _Avoid_: Bullseye, perfect answer

**Tie**: Estimates at the same distance from the solution. They earn the same points. _Avoid_: Draw

**Impostor points**: The 5 points the impostor earns in a round where they escaped. A caught impostor earns nothing for the vote. _Avoid_: Escape bonus

**Catcher bonus**: The 2 points every catcher earns on top of their placement points. _Avoid_: Vote points

**Scoreboard**: Every player of a game with the points they have added up so far, including those who never sent an estimate. _Avoid_: Leaderboard, ranking, standings

**Final ranking**: The scoreboard once the game's last round is over. _Avoid_: Podium, results
