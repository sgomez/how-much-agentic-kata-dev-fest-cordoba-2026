# Impostor

The hunt for each round's impostor. After the reveal it collects the other players' votes and gives the verdict: caught or escaped.

## Language

**Voting**: The phase after the reveal of a round with an impostor, while the other players vote. It ends when every player who can vote has voted or its deadline passes. Rounds without an impostor have no voting. _Avoid_: Poll, ballot

**Vote**: One player's pick of who they think the impostor of a round was. The impostor does not vote, nobody votes for themselves, and a vote is final once sent. _Avoid_: Accusation, suspicion

**Abstention**: A player who could vote and sent no vote in time. It counts for nobody. _Avoid_: Blank vote, null vote

**Caught**: The verdict when the impostor has strictly more votes than any other player. _Avoid_: Found, unmasked

**Escaped**: The verdict when the impostor is not caught, a tie at the top included. _Avoid_: Survived, got away, not found

**Verdict**: Whether a round's impostor was caught or escaped, known once the voting ends. _Avoid_: Result, outcome

**Catcher**: A player who voted for the impostor in a round where the impostor was caught. _Avoid_: Winner, hunter

## Open questions

- **Voting deadline**: how long does the voting last?
- **Who opens the voting**: the host with a button, or automatically after the reveal?
- **What the verdict shows**: only caught or escaped, or also who voted for whom? (Patterns/08 · WhatItShows)
